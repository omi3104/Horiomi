Drop ONE background-music file here (mp3 / m4a / wav) and it will be mixed in
quietly under the voiceover. Leave this folder empty for voice-only videos.

Use only music you are licensed to use. Free options:
  - YouTube Audio Library  (Studio -> Audio library)  - free, cleared for YouTube
  - Pixabay Music  (https://pixabay.com/music/)        - free, no attribution
  - Free Music Archive CC0 tracks
